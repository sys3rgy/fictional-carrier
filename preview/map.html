Isometric battle map · two carriers · three lanes

Kestrel Reach

The arena is a square in world space, so the camera projects it as a diamond — the playfield is isometric, not just the things standing on it. Carriers spawn at opposing corners, and the three routes between them run along the isometric axes: the centre cuts the diagonal, while each flank runs out along one diamond edge, rounds a corner and comes back in along the next. Asteroid and wreckage fields wall those corridors in.

The sun is an actual light source, not a painted highlight: every prop is baked once per light direction and per falloff tier, then placed with the pair matching its real bearing and distance to the sun — the same trick that bakes a ship once per facing. The layout has 180° rotational symmetry about the centre, which maps each spawn corner onto the other.

Isometric sprite set · 8 facings · 6 animations

Lancer-class Carrier

Every frame here is rendered, not drawn. The carrier is described once as a small 3D model of boxes, ellipsoids and cylinders grouped into swappable modules; each animation poses that model, and the pose is rotated in 45° steps and projected through a fixed isometric camera. Swap the hangar module and all 8 facings of all 6 animations agree on proportion, lighting and palette by construction.
